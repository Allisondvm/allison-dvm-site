Clinical practice has shown me where drug safety and medical communication succeed
and where they fall short, because I have watched it play out on real patients. When
a new medication changes how a geriatric patient tolerates routine screening, or when
a subtle adverse event only makes sense once you have seen it three times, that is
knowledge that lives at the exam table. I want to bring that perspective to the teams
that design, monitor, and communicate about the drugs our patients depend on.
